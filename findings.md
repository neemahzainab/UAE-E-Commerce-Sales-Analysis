Q7: Do bigger discounts lead to larger orders?

Result: Average order value decreases as discount percentage increases: ~696 AED with no discount, ~652 AED for 1-10%, ~587 AED for 11-20%, and ~458 AED for 21%+ discounts. All four groups have solid sample sizes 
(4,200-19,000 orders each).
Finding: Contrary to the common assumption that bigger discounts drive bigger purchases, this data shows the opposite, that is, orders with larger discounts tend to have lower average value. This could mean discounts 
are applied more often to already lower-priced items, or that heavily discounted items are typically smaller purchases, rather than discounts actually causing customers to spend less. The data shows a correlation, 
not a proven cause.
